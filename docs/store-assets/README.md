# Store listing media

Canonical store-listing artwork for Drapeon.

- `android/play-phone/`: eight Google Play phone screenshots in listing order (`01` through `08`), 1080 x 1920.
- `ios/iphone-69/`: nine App Store iPhone screenshots in listing order, 1290 x 2796. Uploaded to the iPhone 6.7-inch set for App Store version 1.0.2; the existing 6.5-inch set is retained.
- `preview/drapeon_store_overview.png`: campaign/contact-sheet overview. This is not a store screenshot or a store-link Open Graph image.

Apple and Google generate previews for their own store URLs from the live listing. Drapeon-controlled URLs should use a dedicated 1200 x 630 Open Graph image instead of this overview collage.

## Current listing copy

These are the English (U.S.) listing values prepared for release 1.0.2.

### Apple App Store

- Name: `Drapeon`
- Subtitle: `Find a tailor. Make it yours`
- Keywords: `custom clothing,bespoke,alterations,seamstress,measurements,made to measure,bridal,handmade,style`
- Promotional text: `Find a tailor for your next custom outfit. Explore portfolios, save favorites, share your fit, and keep every order detail in one place.`
- What's New: `A refreshed way to discover tailors, save favorites, and plan custom clothing. Explore updated portfolios, share your fit, and keep your order details together.`

### Google Play

- Short description: `Find trusted tailors for custom clothing, alterations, and ready-made outfits.`
- Full description:

  > Find trusted tailors for custom clothing, bespoke outfits, and alterations.
  >
  > Drapeon is a tailoring marketplace for discovering independent tailors and studios around the world. Explore portfolios, specialties, locations, availability, and ready-made clothing before choosing who fits your style and needs.
  >
  > Plan a custom order in one place:
  >
  > - Share your garment idea, reference photos, fit preferences, timing, and delivery needs
  > - Keep measurements together and reuse them for future briefs
  > - Review the tailor’s quote, tailoring costs, fabric allowance, estimated completion, and delivery details
  > - Keep messages, decisions, and order progress connected from brief through handoff
  > - Save favorite tailors and garments to wishlists
  >
  > Tailors can organize customer measurements in a client diary, collaborate with clients on garment designs, manage custom orders, and sell ready-made pieces.
  >
  > Drapeon supports made-to-measure and ready-to-wear clothing, from wedding outfits and suits to cultural clothing, modest fashion, separates, and alterations. Tailor availability, features, payment methods, and delivery options vary by location and eligibility.

Store URLs generate their own share previews from the store listing. The updated app name, subtitle, icon, and screenshot set are the source for those previews; don't substitute the overview collage.
